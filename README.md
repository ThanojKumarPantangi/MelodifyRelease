# Melodify

**A modern Android music app built for a clean, immersive, and feature-rich listening experience.**

Melodify provides ad-free music streaming with powerful playback controls, synchronized lyrics, offline listening, smart recommendations, music discovery, and real-time listening experiences.

---

## Overview

Melodify delivers a seamless music experience by bringing together powerful playback features, music discovery, personalization, lyrics, offline listening, and intelligent queue management in one application.

The goal of Melodify is simple: **make listening to music faster, cleaner, and more enjoyable.**

---

## Features

### What's New

* **Data Saver Mode** — Reduce data consumption during playback when using limited connections.
* **Settings Search** — Quickly find and navigate to settings options.
* **Redesigned UI** — A cleaner, faster, and more intuitive interface.
* **Podcast Support** — Listen to podcasts alongside your music library.
* **Local Media Support** — Play music stored directly on your device.
* **Dynamic Island Support** — Enhanced playback notifications on supported Android devices.

---

## Streaming & Playback

* **Ad-Free Streaming** — Listen without interruptions.
* **High Quality Streaming** — Adaptive bitrate audio streaming.
* **Data Saver Mode** — Reduce data usage during streaming.
* **Seamless Playback** — Switch between available audio and video playback modes.
* **Background Playback** — Continue listening while using other applications or when the screen is off.
* **Offline Mode** — Download tracks, albums, and playlists for offline listening.
* **Crossfade** — Create smooth transitions between songs.
* **Canvas Animations** — Enjoy visual animations while music is playing.

## Lyrics

Melodify provides an immersive lyrics experience with:

* **Synchronized Lyrics** — Lyrics stay synchronized with playback.
* **Multiple Lyric Animations** — Choose from different lyric presentation styles.
* **Word-by-Word Lyrics** — Precise synchronization at the word level.
* **Lyrics+** — Improved lyrics coverage and accuracy.
* **AI Translation** — Translate lyrics into different languages.
---

## Smart Playback

Melodify provides intelligent playback behavior to make listening more convenient.

* **Pause on Mute** — Automatically pause playback when the device is muted.
* **Resume on Bluetooth** — Resume playback when compatible Bluetooth headphones or earbuds reconnect.
* **Background Playback** — Continue playback while using other applications.
* **Sleep Timer** — Automatically stop playback after a selected duration.

---

## Integrations

* **Music Sharing** — Share songs through cross-platform song links.
* **Local Media** — Access music stored on the device.

## Customization

Melodify provides several options to personalize the listening experience.

* **UI Density Scale** — Adjust interface spacing.
* **High Refresh Rate Support** — Take advantage of supported high-refresh-rate displays.
* **Hide Player Thumbnail** — Keep the player minimal by hiding album artwork.
* **Crop Album Art** — Customize how album artwork is displayed.
* **Hide Video Songs** — Filter video content from music feeds.
* **Hide YouTube Shorts** — Keep Shorts out of music browsing.
